WHO WE ARE

Crafting Spaces That tell
Your Story

Hasta Interior Studio is a full-service interior design firm based in Avinashi, Tirupur. We specialise in transforming residential and commercial spaces into beautifully designed environments that reflect the personality and aspirations of the people who live and work in them.

Founded with a passion for aesthetic living, our team combines creativity with technical precision to deliver interiors that are not just visually stunning, but also highly functional and built to last. Every project is handled with meticulous attention to detail — from the first sketch to the final installation.
