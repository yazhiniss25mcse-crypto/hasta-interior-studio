Living Room Design

Your living room is the centrepiece of your home — where family gathers and guests are welcomed. Hasta Interior Studio crafts living rooms that strike the perfect balance between comfort and elegance, designed around the way you actually live.

From TV unit design and sofa selection to false ceilings, wall panelling, and ambient lighting — we design every element cohesively so your living room tells a unified, beautiful story.

What You Get

  • Full living room layout & space planning
  • Custom TV unit & entertainment wall design
  • False ceiling with ambient lighting plan
  • Sofa, rug & soft furnishing curation
  • Wall paint, texture & panel finish options
  • Design that reflects your personality & style
  • Cohesive look from floor to ceiling
  • Transparent pricing with no hidden costs
  • End-to-end project management
