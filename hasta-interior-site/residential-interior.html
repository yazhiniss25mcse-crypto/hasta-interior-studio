Residential Interior Design

At Hasta Interior Studio, we design complete residential interiors for apartments, independent houses, and villas across Tamil Nadu. Every project begins with understanding your lifestyle, preferences, and budget — and ends with a home that feels uniquely yours.

From space planning and furniture selection to lighting design and material sourcing, our team manages every aspect of your residential interior so you can move into a beautiful, functional home without the stress of managing multiple vendors.

What You Get

  • Complete space planning and layout design
  • Custom furniture & built-in unit design
  • Material, colour palette & finish selection
  • Lighting plan & electrical coordination
  • On-site execution & quality supervision
  • Personalised design tailored to your lifestyle
  • Premium materials & quality workmanship
  • Transparent pricing with no hidden costs
  • End-to-end project management
